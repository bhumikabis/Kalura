Why Kalura Exists

Culture is everywhere. Discovery and trust shouldn’t be fragmented.

Today’s Reality

Where cultural makers and buyers get stuck

01. Dependence on middlemen and fairs: Artisans and cooks struggle for steady year-round visibility.
02. Language and smartphone barriers: Marketplaces expect English typing and digital selling skills.
03. Scattered cultural discovery: Crafts, cuisine, folk arts, and experiences live in separate places.
04. Buyer hesitation on authenticity and hygiene: Without inspection, buyers worry about imitations and kitchen cleanliness.

The Kalura Way

One verified home built for human craft and trust

01. Five linked cultural pillars: Kala, Rasa, Lok, Utsav, and Anubhav united on one platform.
02. Inclusive onboarding—phone or no phone: Odia voice listings via app/WhatsApp, plus local representative visits.
03. AI check + master-artisan sign-off: Verified texture, fair yarn-board pricing, and unique QR provenance.
04. Daily live kitchen proof: Time- and GPS-stamped in-app kitchen captures before you order food.
